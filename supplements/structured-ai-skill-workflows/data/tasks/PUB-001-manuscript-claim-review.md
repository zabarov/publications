# PUB-001: Manuscript Claim Review

## Task Facts

A manuscript section claims:

"The proposed AI skill workflow significantly improves reliability across software engineering tasks and can be used as a general standard for AI-assisted development."

Available evidence:

- one public practitioner article draft;
- internal skill examples;
- no controlled experiment;
- no independent reviewer scoring;
- no public dataset.

The user asks: "Can this claim go into the paper?"

## Known Risks

- effectiveness claim is unsupported;
- general standard claim is premature;
- internal examples may not be publishable evidence;
- no public reproducibility package exists;
- no independent evaluation exists.

## Expected Evidence

- literature comparison;
- experiment results or pilot scoring;
- public-safe dataset;
- limitation statement;
- claim rewrite.

## Expected Output Qualities

- should reject or soften the claim;
- should propose a defensible replacement;
- should distinguish conceptual contribution from empirical result;
- should list evidence required for stronger claims.
