Status: planned.

## Purpose

Give readers a practical decision framework: when to use one multi-specialist
skill, when to route work through a federation of owner skills, and when a
simpler workflow is enough.

## Public Boundary

- Use fictional or sanitized examples.
- Avoid private internal contracts and customer-specific cases.
- Focus on the decision model and trade-offs.
