# PUB-001 / C0_COMPACT

The manuscript claim should be weakened if it presents a pilot result as validation. A self-scored synthetic pilot can support method readiness, rubric usefulness or a rationale for further testing. It cannot support general superiority, statistical improvement or submission-ready empirical claims.

Evidence needed: exact manuscript sentence, study design, scoring method, reviewer independence, task count, prompt-control details and data availability.

Next steps: rewrite the claim to say the pilot suggests the rubric can surface differences and motivates controlled validation. Add limitations for synthetic tasks, self-scoring, prompt length and no blinded review.

Blocker: do not state that the model is validated until independent or blinded scoring exists.
